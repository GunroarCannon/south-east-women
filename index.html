South East Women Initiatives

Founded under divine grace, the South East Women Initiatives (SEWI) stands as a beacon of unity and progress for women across the five Eastern states of Nigeria. As a non-governmental, non-partisan organization, we dedicate ourselves to the empowerment and elevation of women through strategic initiatives in governance, agriculture, education, and healthcare.

Our mission, rooted in the principles of love and equity, drives us to combat societal challenges faced by women while fostering skill development and creating sustainable opportunities. Through collaborative efforts, we aim to establish a legacy of empowered women shaping the future of our communities.
